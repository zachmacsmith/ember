B019 addresses the irreversible partial-placement model: all vertices and original contacts are present from initialization, and ownership can overlap while contact locations change. This is a representation hypothesis, separate from the particular adjacent-edge neighborhood, strict current-price acceptance and its implementation cost.

The four fixed calls have different roles. Path and star at Q=n confirm cheap valid initialization; they provide no overlap-resolution evidence if initially feasible. Triangle on triangle must remove its initial overlap and reach Q3, so it tests an actual construction continuation. Triangle on a physical path checks that a privately connected contact state is never credited as a minor while owners overlap.

Record initial/final Q and O, complete/limited queries, each inspected combination's Q/O/energy and eligibility, accepted changes, price epochs, normalization/initialization/query/finalization work and time. A completed lower-O proposal rejected because its energy does not fall would identify an acceptance restriction **within the inspected neighborhood**. A completed feasible or improving proposal discarded by a query limit identifies cost/completion policy as an obstacle. Many uncompleted distance/pool queries indicate that amortization was insufficient. Complete queries with no useful proposal do not separate bad initial placement from an inadequate neighborhood: they cannot prove the representation lacks a better state.
