# C018 mechanism decision

2026-09-10 UTC. **Reject fixed C018 and retire the old-root ablation. Retain whole-owner relocation as a demonstrated completion capability, not as the retained algorithm. A061 remains retained.** The [complete results](c_018_results.md) satisfy the all-success part of the exploratory rule but fail every paired quality condition: 10/10 regressions against A061 and MM, no positive reference gap closed by one-third, and uniformly poor first-valid quality. The tiny-check PASS and the ablation's 0/10 cannot override those regressions.

The root restriction was a consequential missing neighborhood in this coherent constructor. Across grid, BA, SBM and the known-feasible control, with both seeds and a BA relabel, full roots complete while old roots do not. Full construction exercised 10,840 completed proposals, 5,479 actual geometry changes and **5,156 accepted roots outside the prior owner territory**. Its 1,695 accepted positive-energy proposals broke 1,326 contacts while gaining 4,264. Hence neither nonlocal movement nor uphill acceptance was merely present in pseudocode. The ablation isolates root eligibility within this policy; divergent histories do not prove that every old-root search is incapable of completion. The final-minor representation itself was not excluded: the full version produced valid minors in every case.

Two distinct quality limitations remain, supported by completed receipts rather than a new optimization campaign:

- **Construction proposal quality.** The selected root's singleton score exceeded the best eligible score by median 26, mean 43.04 weighted site-cost units across full-root construction proposals; per-input medians range from 15 to 64.5. This is intentional broad sampling, not a scoring implementation error. All first-valid maps were large (Q570–868), and even 92–147 later reductions per call left every result worse than both references. These observations support poor placement/route compactness as a common deficit; they do not isolate sampling temperature from the singleton proxy or canonical greedy tree growth. Unbuilt alternative roots have no measured final-tree cost, so a lower proxy score is not itself a demonstrated better embedding.
- **Post-validity acceptance and proposal reach.** Of 6,016 completed quality proposals, 1,179 valid shorter maps were accepted. The rule rejected **1,367 valid, equal-Q proposals that changed geometry**, a concrete class of neutral rearrangements it cannot traverse. Another 2,276 proposals were geometrically identical; 767 were valid but longer and 427 were invalid. Thus useful reach is restricted by both admission and what the one-root/canonical-tree proposal produces. Neutral rearrangements may enable later shortening, but the saved records do not show that they do, or that they address gaps of +110 to +297 Q against MM. Accepting them blindly could cycle. This supports at most a bounded complete-constructor test of principled neutral movement if root selects it after the A/B review, not claimed gap closure or a sequence of local repairs. No such test or numerical sweep is authorized by this note.

Cost does not explain why full C018 stopped improving: every full call deliberately exhausted its deterministic quality policy after less than six of the allowed sixty seconds. There were no active work caps. Old roots instead consumed the search allowance, but 98.8% of its publications were identical geometry and millions of neighbor endpoints remained inaccessible. Increasing that allowance, accelerating the same repeated proposals, or citing intermediate contact-distance improvements would not resolve the demonstrated quality deficit. Incumbent validation is measurable cost, but removing its time alone changes neither the rejected neutral moves nor the poor first-valid maps.

The shared missing capability is **compact, mutually compatible contact placement**: completion by relocating connected owners is possible, but distant sampled placements and a single-owner strict-Q descent leave much larger chains on every tested structure. MM's previously observed multi-contact singletons and shared branching paths remain the relevant structural benchmark. C018 allowed arbitrary connected branches and general source graphs; that representational permission was insufficient to realize compact arrangements. The singleton control's large gap makes this conclusion stronger without exposing its witness to any candidate.

Stop this fixed policy's promotion path, the old-root direction and additional routing-kernel/local-repair work justified only by intermediate counts. Do not run the proposed broader continuation or another timeout screen. Preserve C003–C017 rejections and all C018 per-seed regressions. The completion mechanism may inform a future **coherent** design only after the root compares A/B outcomes and chooses a mechanism with a credible path to substantial shared gap closure. That design would need to distinguish productive geometry changes from cycling, address construction compactness as well as later plateau traversal, and be judged promptly by complete constructors on diverse development inputs. This note proposes no implementation, exact solve or new experiment.

Evidence is exploratory: two seeds, four original structures, one relabel and no untouched confirmation exposure. Variance is descriptive; control feasibility is not MM optimality. The final objective remains class-level success and ACL improvement against MM on Z12, allowing optimal ACL-1 ties and preserving every regression. C018 has contributed a useful causal observation, not an algorithm ready for publication.
