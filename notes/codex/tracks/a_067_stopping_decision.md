# A after A067: stop this refinement sequence

2026-09-10 UTC. **Stop further fixed-outside-contact attachment-score and narrow
pair refinements. Keep A061 retained and keep the inherited A research track
available for a materially different proposal after root compares all tracks.**
This is a stopping decision for the present sequence, not proof that local
refinement, contact sharing or coordinated relocation cannot work. No A068
implementation or additional experiment is proposed by this note.

The [complete A067 result](../experiments/067_contact_coverage_results.md) fails
both frozen conditions: only ER96/BA96 improve over both controls among the six
fresh random families, and regular96 loses one qubit to each control. Its
changed attachment rule does expose useful strict-Q moves, including the three
old regression anchors. That supports the mechanism's local relevance. It does
not support another score adjustment: WS96 and singleton-control96 retain all
25 and 71 qubits of their A066/MM excess, every input completes fewer trees than
A066, and all twelve added stages reach their deadlines. A067's six canonical
MM wins/five losses are the same success-conditioned win/loss set as retained
A061 on this cohort. The full package substantially closes A061's wheel gap
from 62 qubits to four and SBM160's from 61 to one; that inherited progress is
preserved. The incremental A067/A066 savings on those inputs are five and one.
No aggregate ER gain can stand in for the unchanged WS/control deficits.

MM's directly inspected capability remains relevant: it can reconstruct whole
chains and relocate contacts without every working-state change strictly
improving the saved incumbent. That capability explains what A's local query
does not currently attempt; saved MM final maps do not reveal that it used a
particular neutral bridge on these inputs. See the pinned-source
[capability review](../stock_mm_capabilities_review.md). MM maps and hidden
control witnesses remain offline evidence only and must never seed a candidate.

Do not replace this stop with a generic “larger joint region” proposal. The
repo already specifies [joint regions](../joint_region_spec.md), and earlier
[joint-path results](../joint_path_reconstruction_results.md) improve only a
cycle under their fixed representation. The
[induced-star review](../induced_star_relocation_review.md) found missed shorter
assignments inside the old region, so expanding that region alone was not the
explanation. Those are different states and policies, not a universal veto;
they nonetheless require a new proposal to identify exactly what contact or
assignment freedom changes. Peer B also distinguishes its failed local
contact/fragment diffusion from valid-incumbent region rerouting, and peer C
is considering joint placement during construction. Root should compare these
proposals before allocating another A implementation. No automatic A/B/C merger
or best-output selection follows.

**Reopening evidence, for a separately reviewed proposal.** State one concrete
restriction being removed, and show why a general operator can remove it at
plausible cost. For example, release actual contact domains of multiple owners
and redistribute chain sites in one private transaction, instead of merely
reconnecting to the same pinned endpoints. This is an example of the required
observation, not a sufficiently specified new algorithm. A tiny actual-coupler
fixture can falsify claimed reach immediately; do not start a broad exact
campaign or use a global IP constructor.

Before code, a future proposal must provide its own concise hypothesis,
pseudocode, one self-critique and cheap falsifier, reconcile the historical
negative mechanisms, and freeze one small complete-constructor screen. Its
decisive evidence must be substantial complete gap closure on several
previously losing structures, including fresh development in different
families, with every success/Q regression retained. A proposed quality target
worth reviewing is at least one-third closure of a positive MM Q gap on two
structurally different fresh instances, plus no regression against the stated
retained/control methods; this is a prospective discussion target, not a new
retroactive A067 rule or sufficient promotion criterion. Preserve regular96 and
the ER160/SBM160/wheel anchors alongside fresh instances. Lower local tree cost,
more contact coverage, more accepted intermediate moves or cheaper queries
alone cannot reopen this sequence.

Track first valid handoff and full incumbent ACL trajectories under the same
host's paired timing, including setup, rejected work, compilation and failures.
Allow wall allocation to be justified by measured useful work; do not impose an
unexplained fixed multiplier or infer a safe cutoff from these retrospective
curves. Any active search limit needs an empirical reason. The Z12 priority,
dependency isolation, no MM/busclique calls, source-family-independent rules,
unchanged validation and single evolving algorithm constraints remain.

**Self-critique of the stop.** All current A067 evidence is one seed, and the
timed neighborhoods are unfinished. A better proposal might make the discarded
late time useful, while stopping score refinements gives up possible small
gains. The counterweight is measured complete quality: a third local extension
has not changed the persistent WS/control deficits or the MM win/loss set, and
it adds a new regression. This supports reallocating design effort now without
claiming mathematical impossibility or ending the overall research objective.
