# Next constructor hypothesis: independent region growth and deletion

**Constructor-level finding.** C006 chooses `min(|H|,4L)` occupied sites, where L is the summed source-degree bound, coarsens them into n regions, then preserves Q through label swaps and owned-site transfers. Deletion begins only after all original contacts exist: see [allocation](../../../packages/ember-qc/src/ember_qc/algorithms/quotient_compact.py#L30), [search](../../../packages/ember-qc/src/ember_qc/algorithms/quotient_reconfiguration.py#L235), and [deletion entry](../../../packages/ember-qc/src/ember_qc/algorithms/quotient_distinct.py#L142). This is a structural restriction, not proof that it uniquely causes the poor final quality.

The earlier experiments narrow the choice. [C001/C002](c_002_results.md) rejected refinement inside fixed parent regions, including provable cut-capacity deficits. [C003/C004](c_004_results.md) showed that reducing initial occupied volume greatly improves sparse Q, but all four successful outputs still lose to MM. [C005](c_005_results.md) crossed the initial boundary without changing Q and gained no completion. [C006/C006b](c_006b_results.md) improved distinct contact capacity but retained the same four failures; sparse final Q remained 313/207/367/457. More time, another fixed allocation factor or another source/target splitting hierarchy would repeat rejected directions.

**Two alternatives; select the second.** One could retain C006's coarsening, delete sites redundant for its currently realized contacts before feasibility, then allow regrowth. That is cheap but preserves the original allocation/assignment and much of its early contact geometry. Instead, start every source vertex as a singleton and let its connected region grow or shrink independently throughout construction. This removes the four-times allocation and target coarsening entirely. It is distinct from A's geometric order/conversion constructor and [B019's](b_019_constructor_policy.md) explicit coupler witnesses with temporary overlap: this state stays disjoint, permits missing contacts, and stores contact multiplicities without selecting a permanent witnessing coupler.

**Selected hypothesis and fixed provisional policy.** Starting at Q=n and charging occupied sites before feasibility may create shorter contact geometry than filling a large quotient and pruning afterward. Use the full original target throughout; no active-domain restriction or reserve invariant survives from the supplied-state diagnostics.

Use degree-first source BFS forest order, with seeded ties. Reuse C004's two-sweep target-center definition and take one seeded target BFS prefix of exactly n sites. Pair the traversal orders injectively. The seeds are movable regions, never compulsory terminals. One seeded RNG supplies the fixed choices; no alternative initialization is tried. Global n/m/degree necessary bounds remain prechecks. An individual partial chain may be shorter than its full-source degree bound; only nonempty connectivity/disjointness are invariant before full-original validation.

Let M be the exact number of missing original edges. Set `E = M + Q/(1+d)`, where d is the second target BFS sweep's eccentricity, computed once. This is a fixed heuristic tradeoff, not a bound on embedding quality. Under one trajectory, cycle through:

```text
missing-contact-directed whole-region label exchange
connected owned-site transfer toward a missing neighbor
independent addition of one free neighboring site toward a missing neighbor
independent nonarticulating-site deletion from a seeded uniformly chosen owner
```

Score at most sixteen eligible proposals per visit; count eligibility scans and sorting too. Use exact ΔM and ΔQ. Select minimum ΔE, then full-target distance for directed site moves, then seeded rank. Accept nonpositive ΔE; otherwise use `exp(-ΔE/0.5)`. Distance caches depend on the destination region and the **full** target, never the current occupied induced graph. Connectivity-preserving transfers/deletions may lose contacts under this energy rule; the complete source contact set is not frozen prematurely. Additions do not require simultaneous releases, and deletions do not require replacement sites. Both signs of ΔQ must occur before feasibility in the targeted mechanism test.

Stop at the first complete original-valid minor, then use the unchanged audited deletion pass and final original-target gate. Bound search by `4|V(H)|` visits and the common deadline minus the existing final-validation reserve; all preparation, caches, rejected work and output costs count. Preserve failed/late states and exact Q/M histories. No previous constructor, MM, busclique, global optimization, family rule or independent best-of selection is called.

**Self-critique and cheap full-construction falsifier.** Singleton placement can be poor or initially sealed; the size penalty can favor erosion that undoes necessary preparatory growth. Full-target distances can point through occupied obstacles and cost too much. Single-site moves and scalar annealing are established ideas, so novelty is unproved. The proposal tests a changed construction trajectory, not a new name for those operations.

Before a panel, test exact signed Q/contact deltas, partial-state growth/deletion and deadlines, then full constructors on path3→path5, star4→the same star, triangle→cycle6, and triangle→path3. Require valid Q3/Q4/Q6 on the positives and no embedding credit on the negative at seed 0 within five seconds each. The cycle forces actual growth beyond the singleton start. If these pass, propose the same eight exposed Z12 inputs, seed 0, fifteen seconds per arm, fresh paired MM on hyde04. Reject this fixed policy if it loses a C006 sparse success, or obtains neither a new harder completion nor lower Q on at least three of the four sparse successes. Any individual MM win remains a separate observation, not a family-mean or generalization claim. This is design only; no code or constructor call has run.

## Pre-code enumeration, deadline and diagnosis clarification

A single `random.Random(seed)` first shuffles sorted source ranks once. Degree-first BFS roots and newly discovered neighbors use `(-degree, shuffled_rank)`; the resulting forest traversal is paired with the target BFS discovery order. The target's largest component and two-sweep center use C004's deterministic physical-rank ties. Target BFS shuffles each sorted unseen-neighbor list; it stops at exactly n discovered sites. Choosing the largest component is an initialization limitation, not an infeasibility theorem for disconnected sources.

Visits cycle swap, transfer, addition, deletion, beginning with swap. Each directed visit draws one index uniformly from the maintained missing-edge array (initially sorted; exact old swap-delete maintenance thereafter) and one fair endpoint bit. For swap, enumerate source owners other than the chosen endpoints that already touch the destination chain, shuffle their increasing-rank list, then score its first sixteen. There is no partial-chain degree floor. For transfer/addition, enumerate distinct occupied-other/free sites adjacent to the chosen source chain, shuffle the increasing-site list once, then sort by full-target distance to the destination chain and that shuffled rank. A transfer must leave a nonempty connected donor; additions need only that boundary adjacency. Score the first sixteen eligible sites. Deletion draws an owner uniformly, shuffles its increasing-site list, and scores the first sixteen whose removal leaves a nonempty connected chain. All enumeration, sorting and connectivity work is charged; the sixteen limit bounds scores, not scans. Complete all available scored candidates before choosing. Ties use distance only for directed site moves, then the assigned shuffled rank. Draw one uniform acceptance variate only when the selected ΔE is positive; no draw for empty visits or nonpositive ΔE. Cache entries are keyed by destination owner and its geometry version, incremented by every swap/transfer/addition/deletion affecting that owner.

Let `D=min(external_deadline, call_start+timeout)` and `r=min(0.5 s,0.1*timeout)`. Input, initialization, search and unchanged deletion cleanup share `D-r`; search additionally stops after exactly `4*|V(H)|` completed visits. There is no dedicated cleanup allocation or restart. First complete contact state must pass original validation before cleanup. If cleanup reaches `D-r`, retain its last safely committed chains. Full final validation, output copying and status bookkeeping share D; any final crossing is TIMEOUT with late output retained only diagnostically. Search exhaustion retains its actual partial-contact map, Q/M and stop reason, never credits an embedding. Interruption during a visit retains the scored prefix, not a falsely completed visit.

The representation change is removing fixed Q/coarsening, while independent addition/deletion also changes the move neighborhood: their effects are confounded in this first falsifier. Save initial/final and per-visit Q/M, selected/scored ΔM/ΔQ/ΔE, acceptance draws and outcomes, eligibility rejects/empty visits, cache invalidations, stage time and work, including interrupted prefixes. Persistent missing contacts with no eligible moves implicate this neighborhood/initial placement; many rejected contact-improving or preparatory additions implicate the fixed acceptance tradeoff; accepted growth immediately undone by deletion indicates destructive scheduling/acceptance interaction. Useful accepted progress ending at the deadline with distance/eligibility work dominant implicates cost; exhausting the finite visit cap quickly implicates the trajectory, not slow execution. These are diagnostic observations, not causal proofs. The four tiny calls can reject the policy; success only authorizes proposing a diverse full-constructor screen, not claiming that the mechanisms are disentangled. Optimal ACL=1 ties are acceptable.
