The existing state check still validates every owner as a connected disjoint

this reader introduces no route solver.

The changed summary follows the constructor's mechanisms:

- Construction and quality phases remain separate. Preserve first-valid Q/time,
  every strict-Q event, the latest certified equal-Q/R state, terminal stop and
  complete phase costs. An evaluator derives the first recorded time reaching
  A061's final Q; that threshold never enters the constructor.
- Count every scalar visit, complete/unfinished query, accepted versus committed
  proposal, actual geometry change, contact change and signed Delta Q/M/E/R.
  Preserve selection/growth obstructions separately from interruption. Counters
  and sweep publications are checked for internal consistency, not credited as
  additional embeddings.
- Show selected-leaf distributions and long/degree exclusions in each phase and
  its first/last observed sweep, including interrupted sweeps. These are repeated
  visits and observations of singleton attrition, not unique-owner counts or a
  proof about another representation.
- Report the saved actual-assignment cost above its same-footprint lower bound:
  root cost only where the root already covers all leaves and its actual score
  was recorded, the last selected extension, and the observed per-visit maximum.
  These are **not** regret against another root/tree or a predicted constructor
  improvement. No-assignment visits do not supply observed zero gaps.

Success/mean ACL/two-seed sample variance/within-chain variance and all-attempt
wall/CPU/process time reuse the audited rows. Gap records cover **both** candidate
arms against A061/MM, including missing candidate success and every Q regression.
Relabels remain separate encodings of one topology. First-valid thresholds and
internally valid maps never rescue a failed/late benchmark outcome; only the
original audit assigns credit. Absent diagnostics and missing times remain
explicit, not zeros. R on an invalid state is total required-edge couplers minus
the full number of source edges, so it may be negative.

Outputs are limited to the summary, 24 mechanism receipts, 24 encoding/method
statistics and 24 gap records. Full scalar visits stay in their bound raw files.
No extra projection or validation framework is planned. The exact source diff
