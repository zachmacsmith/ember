# Compound transfer implementation contract

Pre-code clarification of the [approved constructor proposal](c_compound_transfer_constructor_proposal.md): one attempt starts when a transfer visit first encounters a nonsingleton articulation donor and begins new interior-site screening. Its 25,000-record/0.05-second allowance includes eligibility checks through the first eligible site's bridge, compensation and final local certificate. A no-eligible result consumes one of the 128 attempts. Singleton donors never start an attempt. Further interior sites may be screened only until the first fully eligible site is selected; a failed bridge or compensation ends that visit's attempt. Original connected proposals remain available throughout the original ordered 16-score prefix.

The local deadline bounds candidate construction and certification. After a timely private certificate, ordinary selection and publication are charged to the unchanged overall search deadline; neither performs further repair. A local limit cannot discard an already scored ordinary candidate. A global deadline still terminates the original search. Record screening, local limits, certification, selection and commit separately.

The fixed score cap counts both ordinary and compound candidates. A successful compound score can therefore displace the sixteenth ordinary score that the unchanged control would have examined. This is an explicit prefix-coverage effect of the new neighborhood under a shared score cap, not exact replay after a compound candidate exists. The focused checks include a 17-site frontier exhibiting this displacement. Exact ordinary/RNG replay is claimed only when no compound candidate is available. The private construction finish timestamp is idempotent; later ordinary scoring does not inflate its recorded local wall time.

Only private donor/recipient sets and changed physical ownership/contact deltas are staged. All old and incidental contacts after reconnection remain required during the single deletion pass. Before returning a candidate, independently recount affected physical couplers from old and proposed ownership, check the two final regions' connectivity/disjointness, require nonincreasing Q and selected-edge gain, then observe the local/global deadlines. Publication updates ownership, Q, symmetric coupler counts, missing-edge indexing and both geometry versions as one bounded transaction. The unchanged final original-target validator remains authoritative.

The new standalone module loads hash-bound atomic/common implementation bytes from its own source directory. It imports no `results/` helper, stock embedder or package initializer. It executes one evolving constructor; the unchanged atomic arm is a separate experimental control, never a fallback trajectory. New diagnostics do not change RNG draws; path and deletion ties use ascending physical IDs.

Focused checks will cover private compensation/contact deltas and both-owner cache invalidation, rejection/no-mutation and bounded failure paths, exact ordinary proposal/RNG replay when no compound candidate is available, and deadline rejection before publication. No saved C007 state is called again.
