zero observed occurrences. A missing dictionary denotes unknown counts. A tick

certify completed attachment decisions. Whole-stage coverage per site is a
mechanism diagnostic, not the quality metric. Pair and compiled/query intervals
are nested in stage/call time and must never be added again.
