# Fragmentation gate: two compact local witnesses, one lost observation

The retained results establish **two useful compound moves**, not a need to publish disconnected chains. Of 64 sampled contact-preserving fragmented transfers, two reconnected and restored entry Q; 27 had no free reconnection and 35 remained inflated by 1–12 sites. All 24 eligible connected controls also gained contacts at unchanged Q. Neither fragmented witness completes its original source.

The eight-state gate is **incomplete**. Path/tree records were saved normally. After the grid call returned, the driver compared Python tuple edges with JSON list edges and failed before saving that observation. The raw traceback, invocation and original program remain intact; grid outcomes and costs are unknown, not negative. An additive normalization/write-before-check correction then processed only the five unattempted inputs. No state was repeated. The requested schema check passed after that remaining-only run had started; its chronology is explicit in the evidence.

On complete, transferring site 307 from owner 22 to 79 gains two original contacts. A two-site bridge reconnects the donor; two deletions give **M4988→4986, Q618→620→618**. On SBM, site 430 moves from 90 to 22, a three-site bridge and three deletions give **M207→206, Q377→380→377**. Their transfer ΔE values are −1 and 0 for M+C+Q/26; subsequent stages decrease E. These moves were excluded by donor connectivity, not by an uphill acceptance decision. This does not prove that connected-only trajectories lack other routes; the successful controls demonstrate alternatives at these same starts.

Every retained complete certificate passed a separate original-label/target-edge recount, including the inflated outputs. All 88 retained queries finished within their one-second and common fifteen-second deadlines. Seven known input calls took 5.434 seconds total; grid time is missing. Reconnection recount and final certification account for about 3.19 seconds of that measured diagnostic work. No constructor or MM speed comparison follows from these timings.

The evidence supports proposing a **private transfer/reconnect/Q-compensation move between connected published states**. It does not support global fragmentation, higher budgets or another supplied-state search.
