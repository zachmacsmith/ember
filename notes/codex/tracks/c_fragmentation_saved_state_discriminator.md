# One gate for temporary donor fragmentation

**Proposed policy; no implementation or diagnostic calls.** C007 accepted all 640 scored atomic paths but found no free path on 3,700 completed addition visits. Elementary growth already accepted 92.6% of uphill additions. The relevant hypothesis is therefore a restricted move neighborhood: an interior owned site may supply a missing contact even when unused routing cannot. A disconnected donor alone is not useful evidence; its repair must retain that contact and restore connected chains without increasing final Q.

Use the **eight saved atomic-regions final maps**, in the fixed C007 input order, from [the accepted C007b archive](../../../results/codex/retrieved/hyde03/track-c-007b-atomic-growth), bound by its [review manifest](../../../results/codex/track-c-007-review/review_manifest.json). Path/tree remain explicit complete/no-query rows. The six failed maps are partial-contact assignments, not original minors. No elementary map, unsaved best state, MM output or independent query result replaces a start.

## Exact independent-query policy

Each input gets **15 seconds including setup, classification, selection, copies, queries and final checks**. Artifact decoding/hash verification is evaluator preparation outside that clock. No state is published back to the entry.

1. Independently validate chain structure and recount all realized original contacts P. Compute components of the unused **full Z12** target. Classify every missing original edge by whether its endpoint chains touch a common unused component. Only edges with no common component are eligible; retain every excluded edge and reason.
2. Enumerate distinct transfers `(recipient u, donor d, site q)`: q belongs to d, touches u's chain, and touches at least one missing original neighbor of u whose edge passed step 1. Deduplicate the physical transfer even if it gains several edges; its selected edge is the smallest eligible gained edge. Recount contacts exactly. Require a nonempty donor and preservation of P immediately after transfer. Record rejected contact loss, empty donor and all donor-component counts. The two compared classes are donor−q connected and donor−q having **exactly two** components; larger splits are counted but unsupported.
3. Complete this universe before sampling. Within each class take at most **16** transfers ordered by SHA256 of `fragmentation-gate-v1:<source-record-sha256>:<u>:<d>:<q>` (UTF-8 decimal IDs); break hash ties by `(u,d,q)`. Interleave connected then fragmented ranks. No ranking uses reconnection, deletion or energy outcomes. If classification/sorting is interrupted, retain its censored counts and run no queries for that input.
4. Each query starts from a private entry copy and has at most **one second**, clipped by the common deadline. A connected-donor query needs no reconnection. For a fragmented donor, order the two components by minimum physical site and try **one shortest unused path** joining them: sorted starting frontier, sorted physical adjacency, FIFO BFS, first goal. Every other owner, including transferred q, is blocked. Add the entire path to the donor or record no route. No alternative path or additional ownership move is allowed.
5. Record raw reconnection Q and all newly realized original contacts. If Q exceeds entry Q, make one canonical deletion pass restricted to donor and recipient, owners then sites ascending, at most **64 site visits**. Stop when Q reaches entry Q or the pass ends. Each deletion must preserve nonempty connected chains and **all original contacts present after reconnection**, including incidental gains. Do not revisit earlier sites, alter other owners or delete before reconnection. The connected control has no added Q and skips this pass.
6. Independently recount and validate the resulting assignment against the exact original target and required original-contact subgraph. Credit a local witness only if every chain is connected/disjoint/nonempty, every entry and gained contact survives, the selected missing edge is realized, and **Q≤entry Q before both deadlines**. Also report actual remaining original missing edges; local credit is not full-minor success. Preserve late/invalid/raw maps as diagnostics. Discard every private query afterward.

The existing [free-path and local safe-deletion primitives](../../../results/codex/track-c-free-path-diagnostic/diagnose.py) and [original-edge oracle](../../../results/codex/042-results-review/pre-execution/ember_embedding_oracle.py) suffice; their sequential driver is not called. Required-contact adjacency for deletion is the recounted subgraph, never the incompletely realized full source. This is one transfer/reconnection/pruning proposal per query, not a sequence of independent repairs.

## What the result would distinguish

Report all eight inputs, all missing/blocked/excluded edges, full or censored candidate counts, both class sizes, sampled/unsampled queries, and every query outcome. Keep setup, components/contact recount, BFS, deletion, independent gate and total wall time plus examined-site/adjacency/deletion counts. Unstarted and interrupted queries stay in the fixed sampled denominator.

- **Neighborhood:** a certified fragmented witness is rejected by the connected rule at that exact articulation transfer. Connected controls reveal whether ordinary useful transfers also exist. This is a local neighborhood difference, not a proof that a connected trajectory cannot prepare another route.
- **Acceptance:** record exact M/C/Q after transfer, reconnection and pruning, and ΔE for `E=M+C+Q/26`. P-preserving two-component transfers gain at least one contact, so their immediate ΔE is nonpositive. No annealing draw or acceptance ablation occurs; this cannot establish that fragmentation improves full-search acceptance.
- **Access/quality:** no bridge means interior transfer merely moves the connectivity obstruction. A bridge that requires extra final Q fails the compactness gate. Pruning savings can exploit existing redundancy, so report them separately from the transfer and bridge.
- **Cost:** timed-out setup/queries or expensive zero-witness searches count against the mechanism; their unseen outcomes remain unknown.

**Falsifier and self-critique.** If this one bounded gate yields no certified fragmented witness, retire this fixed fragmentation direction rather than change sampling, paths, caps or acceptance. A witness justifies considering one small complete-constructor screen, not successive supplied-state repairs. The gate intentionally excludes temporary contact loss, splits into three or more components and alternate reconnections; a negative result is not a theorem against all disconnected representations. A positive result may still reflect a poor initial geometry or redundant chains and does not establish novelty, better ACL or a useful global search schedule. Tiny checks, if implementation is approved, should cover articulation versus connected controls, lost-contact rejection, reconnection with/without Q compensation, private rollback and late-certificate rejection; they are correctness checks, not the central mechanism evidence.
