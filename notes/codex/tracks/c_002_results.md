The additional diagnostic counts **original source edges**, rather than merely boolean quotient adjacency. For current source aggregates A and B allocated disjoint target regions R_A and R_B, any refinement that keeps each original source vertex inside its allocated region requires

`number of source edges between A and B <= number of target couplers between R_A and R_B`.

Each physical coupler can witness at most one original source edge because its endpoints ultimately have unique source owners. Deleting unused sites cannot create additional crossing couplers. Thus a positive deficit proves that the saved fixed allocation cannot be refined into the original minor, regardless of later split search. This is a necessary condition only; absence of a deficit does not prove a refinement exists. The bounded recount uses the complete saved partial partitions and exact original edges, without exploring alternate partitions or making an infeasibility claim about the source on Z12.

Six of eight saved allocations already violate this condition. For example, the grid has an aggregate pair requiring five original edges but only two physical couplers; one random-planar pair requires four and has one. A regular-graph pair requires 71 and has only three. The complete graph has 18 deficient pairs, with summed deficit 1777. All these states nevertheless satisfy boolean coarse adjacency, explaining why earlier validation accepted them. No amount of later subdivision confined to those regions repairs these deficits.

Path and tree have no pairwise cut deficit, but their local transfers still stop with inaccessible required contacts. On the path, child A grows to 1664 of its parent's 1712 sites while one required contact remains inaccessible under donor preservation. The feasible-seed filter and soft-balance change do take effect: impossible seeds are excluded, and six splits across the panel are accepted below their preferred resource size. Neither change addresses the central restriction on moving across previously chosen boundaries.

Decision: stop tuning this fixed-region formulation. Carry forward two lessons into a substantially different multilevel constructor: original edge-cut multiplicities must survive aggregation, and coarse placement must guide later allocation rather than impose irrevocable physical boundaries. The next candidate will be specified and critiqued before implementation and will initially retain this same panel and budget.
