# C019 mechanism decision

2026-09-10. **Reject the fixed weighted and cardinality policies. Stop the current assumption that repeated exact placement of a center and its currently singleton neighbors, along one root/growth trajectory against soft outside obligations, is sufficient for a general constructor.** This is a decision about the exercised construction architecture, not a proof that connected disjoint chain states or joint contact placement are intrinsically inadequate. A061 remains retained. All C014–C018 and B030 regressions remain rejected; the single C019 grid gain does not revive them. Complete outcomes and bindings are in [the result note](c_019_results.md).

## What the experiment resolved

Exact costed boundary assignment has a real conditional capability: it produces three complete embeddings where the same constructor with cardinality-only assignment produces none. Grid seed 0 reaches Q164 versus A061 Q168, but seed 1 reaches Q165 versus A061 Q161; grid mean ACL ties A061 and remains worse than MM. SBM completes only seed 0 and regresses against both references. There is no two-seed, two-structure gap closure, and no valid result on WS, BA or the singleton control. The fixed falsifier is decisively failed.

This was not a failure to exercise the mechanism. Weighted construction completed **120,101 proposals in 120,110 visits**, with **11,124 actual geometry publications**, including 5,257 with the selected root outside the old center. Cardinality completed 122,359 proposals and 7,300 publications. Every completed weighted block covered its selected singleton leaves; recorded precoverage growth is zero in both arms. The local physical star was constructible, while the remaining original contacts repeatedly prevented a full minor.

Weighted cost optimization also avoids much of the ablation's destructive placement: across proposed blocks, weighted contact losses/gains are 279,669/86,448, versus cardinality 943,378/332,850. These are repeated proposals, not distinct source edges or independent matched-state comparisons. The ablation changes subsequent states and weights. Its zero successes cannot establish the contribution of weighted assignment to compact final quality on structures that neither arm completes.

Proxy gaps are weighted same-footprint assignment costs, not actual alternative-tree regret. Boundary exhaustion after coverage is a query stop that returns its covered block, not a failed complete embedding certificate.

## End-to-end trajectory and next allocation

Only three calls enter quality search, for 2.444s total visit wall. Grid seed 0 admits two strict Q improvements and one equal-Q/R improvement. Across all three quality phases there are 480 proposals: 413 identical geometries, 39 changed equal-Q proposals without the required R improvement, 22 invalid, three longer, two shorter and one equal-Q/R improvement. The latter three publish. Grid seed 1 and SBM seed 0 terminate at their first-valid Q. Thus successful placement is already relatively compact compared with C018's much larger first maps, but the resulting local optimum still misses the reference quality. That historical comparison is descriptive across different frozen screens, not a causal ablation.

The dominant deficit is **construction completion after substantial local contact rearrangement**, followed by quality failure in two of the three complete rows. Soft geometric obligations plus currently singleton joint leaves do not maintain enough coordinated freedom to finish heterogeneous and constrained sparse structures. The control result is especially adverse: even a source with a hidden singleton witness, many multi-leaf moves and ample free target sites remains far from contact-complete. The witness was never supplied to the constructor.

Do not spend the next cycle on additional C019 acceptance tuning, weighted-matching speedups, extra alternative-root trials or a local exact repair chain. Those are not supported as a substantial class-spanning solution by this screen. Preserve costed actual-boundary assignment as a reusable conditional operator and the grid seed 0 complete gain as a lesson. A replacement must change how several source owners obtain compatible positions while their chains grow, with a concrete route to both completion and compactness; merely keeping more singleton leaves or making the same scans faster is not that explanation. No replacement implementation or follow-up experiment is authorized or launched by this note.
