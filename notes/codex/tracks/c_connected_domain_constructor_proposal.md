# Replacement C direction: coordinate connected-chain alternatives

**Design only; no implementation or calls.** Retire C008. Its nine Q-neutral compound commits improved one completion but left five failures; 859/908 attempts found no eligible interior transfer. The new routine occupied only 0.459 seconds of 90.223 solver seconds. All three completed outputs still lost Q to MM, including the two ACL=1 opportunities. The evidence implicates restricted useful proposals and expensive shared searches; it does not establish that connected/disjoint representations or acceptance alone are responsible. Equal initialization controls the comparison without showing that the initial placement is good. [C008 evidence](c_008_results.md).

**Hypothesis.** Keeping several alternative connected chains for every source vertex, and coordinating their contacts and competing occupancy before committing to one full assignment, can avoid freezing poorly placed neighbors. This replaces the construction state and update mechanism. It is not another local ownership repair, C001/C002's hard partition, or B001/B002's irreversible insertion. B considered this direction but chose movable coupler witnesses instead; that [unimplemented alternative](b_construction_replacement_review.md) has not been tested here.

Each vertex v has at most eight distinct, nonempty, connected candidate chains D_v and probabilities p_v. Domains are alternatives within one search, never independently constructed full embeddings. A physical coupler, rather than shared ownership, defines contact. For each candidate C, compute `cost(C)=|C|+lambda*(expected missing original contacts + expected shared sites with other owners)`. Simultaneously update probabilities to half their previous value plus half the softmax of negative costs. Use six epochs, eight updates per epoch, temperature one, and lambda 4,8,16,32,64,128. These are proposed fixed settings, not tuned results or a convergence guarantee.

```text
Create eight seeded, target-wide singleton alternatives per source vertex.
For each epoch under one absolute deadline and total work bound:
    compute exact candidate contact/intersection tables;
    perform eight simultaneous probability updates from the previous weights;
    select one highest-probability chain per vertex, ties by size/seeded rank;
    independently test that whole map; retain a timely valid incumbent if any;
    unless this is the last epoch, generate at most four new alternatives per vertex;
    retain the incumbent's chain and best old/new alternatives, total at most eight.
Return only a timely independently validated incumbent, with one safe deletion pass.
```

Domain generation uses the whole target, with **no frozen chains or blocked owned sites**. Rank roots by expected contact with neighboring domains minus expected other-owner occupancy; inspect four, with fixed seeded ties. Grow one tree per root, at most degree(v) paths: choose a logical neighbor having no contact with any of its domains, then source rank. A multi-source weighted search stops at the first site adjacent to any such domain; own sites cost zero, others cost one plus expected occupancy. Recount contacts after each union. Emit one tree per root, choosing its intermediate state by fewest unsupported neighbors, then current expected cost and size. This does not claim compatibility with one simultaneous neighbor assignment. Retain the incumbent's chain and the lowest-cost distinct old/new alternatives, at most eight. No coupler is a permanent obligation; all failed/discarded growth and selection work count. No exact assignment, global integer solve, MM/busclique, constructor fallback or family rule is used.

**Self-critique.** Finite domains may omit the needed geometry. Independent probabilities can predict mutually inconsistent contacts, and their most probable choices can overlap or miss edges; soft agreement is never an embedding certificate. Simultaneous updates may oscillate. Growing against several alternative neighbors can overallocate Q or repeatedly regenerate identical chains. Rebuilding compatibility tables can dominate runtime. This is a conventional approximate constraint-coordination idea with unestablished novelty. It changes both representation and proposal generation, so a failure will not identify a single cause without the recorded evidence.

**Cheap complete-constructor falsifier, proposed for review.** After only connectivity/contact, probability-update and deadline/non-credit checks, use all eight unchanged C inputs, seed0, fifteen seconds, candidate/fresh MM on the same host: sixteen calls, no supplied-state gate. Cap the entire candidate at 20 million explicitly counted adjacency, pair-compatibility and probability-update records, including setup; reserve the final 0.5 seconds for output/deletion/validation. Continue only with at least **6/8 timely valid**, Q no larger than MM on at least **three common timely inputs** (ACL=1 ties eligible), and median common-pair solver-time ratio at most **3**. Retain every failure. Log domain sizes/deduplication, expected versus selected-map missing/overlap totals, first feasible Q, final Q and stage costs: absent contact-compatible domains implicate generation; support that fails to coordinate implicates selection; incomplete epochs implicate cost. None alone proves global infeasibility. Passing permits broader development evaluation; it is not confirmation of family-level superiority. No calls or implementation are authorized by this note itself.
