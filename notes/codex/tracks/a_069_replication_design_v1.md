# A069 proposal: replicate the shared construction mechanism first

2026-09-10 UTC. **Before-code design only; no implementation, input generation,
reader change or execution authorized.** A068's fixed policies remain rejected;
A061 remains retained. This proposes one complete experiment, not a successor
algorithm or promotion attempt.

**Decision and hypothesis.** Prefer seed/relabel replication over more asks.
Both A068 variants improve A061 on eight shared canonical structures, including
grid, honeycomb, WS and SBM. That is substantive complete-constructor evidence
for their shared active-arm/corner/pricing change. Yet full footprint loses
24 qubits on BA100 and 16 on the BA96 relabel; booking loses 7 there. Test
whether the shared benefit repeats under new solver randomness while those
deficits remain explicit. The persistent target is sparse-graph compactness
and BA placement sensitivity, not lower raw conversion Q alone.

All entered A068 layouts reached 1000 asks, sometimes with late improving
bookmarks. This establishes an active limit, not that more asks repair a
regression. Replication is the more informative next allocation: distinguish
a repeatable construction effect from one favorable seeded trajectory before
buying longer trajectories. It deliberately leaves saturation unresolved.

**One frozen screen.** Reuse ideal Z12 and seven existing development encodings:
g0013 grid128, g0014 honeycomb190, g0102 BA100, g0302 BA96, g0309 its relabel,
g0304 WS96 and g0011 SBM160. Six independent structures, five ordinary families;
the relabel adds no vote. Use fresh solver seeds 1/2, unchanged footprint,
unchanged booking, A061 and MM: **56 complete cold calls**, paired on hyde06.
Both variants are evaluated separately across the entire panel; never choose
their outputs by input. Seed 0 results remain visible beside the new cohort.

```text
freeze the existing four methods, seven encodings and seeds {1,2}
for each fixed graph/seed block, in a frozen balanced method order:
    run each method once under the original common deadline
    preserve every status, returned map, time and existing diagnostic
after all calls terminate:
    reuse original-minor audit; compare complete outputs by graph and seed
    report each fixed arm separately, with aliases and seed0 separated
```

**Allocation.** Keep 1000 asks solely as controlled replication exposure. Both
new arms retain `D−min(10 seconds, remaining/2)` for geometry under the 60s outer
deadline; all surviving time passes to the unchanged later stages. This is
not a final default or an instruction to spend 60s. A068 F geometry took
4.101–13.344s; complete downstream work after layout was at most 6.959s, so
the 10s reserve has measured support with modest margin. New seeds may exceed
it; preserve reserve/geometry/outer stops and simultaneous flags. Keep later
group/work limits unchanged for this causal comparison, explicitly recording
their known early stops. No extra ask-allocation arm or universal MM multiplier.

**What observations distinguish the explanations?** Repeated complete gains
with both booking policies support their shared representation change; repeated
opposite behavior between a graph and its relabel indicates unresolved seeded
placement/search sensitivity. Low raw/core Q followed by worse full handoff,
as on BA100, points to the inadequacy of local compactness for downstream
refinement/lifting; an unreduced BA96 regression excludes lifting as its sole
cause. Existing layout scores/bookmarks, core endpoints and handoff receipts
can locate where ordering reverses, but do not separately prove move reach,
surrogate or acceptance-rule causation. Actual clock stops identify a runtime
constraint; a 1000-ask stop alone does not. No new exact solve or saved-map campaign.

**Cheap falsifier and self-critique.** The BA100/BA96/relabel blocks directly
challenge the assumption that the mechanism generalizes. Repeated regressions
there can reject it without inventing another local metric. Two new seeds are
still a small, selected development sample; count-matched exposure can conceal
unequal work per ask, and this experiment cannot answer whether more asks help.
Do not reinterpret a negative replication as automatic permission to extend
the budget. Complete all 56 frozen calls; no adaptive graph/seed replacement.

**Continuation or stop.** For one *fixed* new arm, require timely validity on
all 14 new calls and no per-call Q loss against A061, including BA100, BA96 and
the relabel. Require at least one-third mean closure of a positive paired
A061-to-MM Q gap in two ordinary families, with improvement over A061 at both
new seeds on each qualifying structure. Report within-chain variance, sample
across-seed ACL variance and all costs separately. The first-screen regressions
remain rejected evidence even if new means improve; this condition supports
only continued mechanism research. If neither arm qualifies, stop this package
at the current representation instead of adding score repairs or buying longer
search to rescue these examples. Any later allocation test needs a separate
decision and freeze after the other tracks' completed results are considered.
