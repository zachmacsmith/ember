# B030 decision: stop contact and fragment diffusion

2026-09-10. **Retire B030 and its connected ablation. Do not continue with a
distance-kernel acceleration, temperature change or larger allowance justified
only by lower contact debt.** The [complete results](b_030_results.md) show
zero first-valid incumbents, zero completions and substantial remaining contact
deficits on every tested structure. This rejects the fixed architecture's
practical premise; it does not prove all disconnected-state algorithms fail.

The hypothesis was that joint transport of existing contact-bearing pieces,
followed by actual free-space reconnection, would create compact compatible
contacts. Those moves occurred. Full B030 accepted 507 PATCH proposals, with
net ΔM=−251, ΔP=+473 and ΔQ=0. Seventy accepted patches improved M while
increasing P. LABEL accepted 3,414 proposals, net ΔM=−1,070, ΔP=+805 and
ΔQ=+1,215; 146 simultaneously improved M and increased P. These are useful
local contact changes, not successful embedding quality.

MERGE accepted 613 proposals and reduced component excess by 618, with net
ΔM=−301 and ΔP=−1,018. It added **1,747 actual sites**, approximately 2.85 per
accepted merge. The 741 recorded proposed merge costs range from 1 to 11,
median 3. Another 147 completed free-domain searches exhausted their domains;
that is current-domain failure, not impossibility on Z12. Reconnection was
neither absent nor universally blocked, but it was expensive in Q. Nevertheless,
no recorded state ever had all source contacts: this is not merely a constructor
waiting to connect its final fragments.

Full-arm recorded slot wall is PATCH 488.239 s, LABEL 133.268 s and MERGE
42.911 s; corresponding slot CPU is 487.920 / 133.228 / 42.900 s. These costs
overlap the exclusive phase totals and must not be added to them. The full
distance cache recorded 39,889 completed generic field calls, 39,880 created
fields and 580,841 cache hits. Imports, graph copies, packing, cache work,
rejected proposals and finalization remain charged. The sixteen-plus-last
detailed site sample is not an exhaustive geometric trace; scalar receipts
cover all recorded slots. No proposed acceleration has measured constructor
quality, and no early-stop speedup follows from this screen.

The contrast with C018 is informative but not a cross-host timing comparison.
Its nonlocal whole-owner reconstruction completed 10/10 versus old roots 0/10,
demonstrating a completion capability absent here. Yet its first-valid Q570–868
and every final Q loss show that moving owners far enough is insufficient for
compactness. MM's previously diagnosed multi-contact singletons and shared
branches remain the structural target. On B's current panel, retained A061's
mean Q gaps of +32 on grid, +10 on SBM and +11.5 on the branch control identify
several deficits that a general compatible-contact mechanism should address.
The BA seed-1 loss and relabel sensitivity remain alongside its ER/BA mean gains.

**Prior work prevents an automatic replacement.** Explicit moving source-edge
couplers with connected owner trees were already B018–B022; fixed outside
endpoints restricted early patches, and whole-incidence release still did not
produce adequate complete constructors. B023 permitted contact loss/restoration
and completed 6/9, but sparse Q was roughly two to three times MM. B024's better
placement proxy, B025's stricter energy and B026–B029's reconstruction/compiled
local-root sequence did not establish class-spanning quality. B029 specifically
lowered every initial overlap while increasing every initial Q, then regressed
both old successes. B030 changed ownership invariants and still failed outright.
These results cannot be reset by renaming soft contact prices or joint movement.

A preliminary coarse-to-fine suggestion is also **not selected**: C001/C002
already attempted source contraction followed by connected region splits,
both 0/8; coarse boolean contacts concealed insufficient original-edge boundary
capacity. Merely permitting expansion or starting with fewer occupied sites
does not supply a concrete new solution to that failure. C018 already covers
nonlocal single-owner relocation. I am therefore recommending the decisive stop
option rather than labeling one of those ideas a new B031 constructor.

No next B implementation or saved-state campaign is proposed in this decision.
A replacement must directly create several mutually compatible contacts at
low actual Q, preserve original-edge obligations through any abstraction, and
define reachable coordinated moves rather than infer success from a distance
sum. It needs its own hypothesis, pseudocode, self-critique and cheap diverse
complete-constructor falsifier before code. Current A/C coordinated-region
proposals can be reviewed independently; their outputs must never be combined
as a portfolio. B030's negative result and every earlier regression remain
part of the research record, while A061 remains the retained algorithm.
