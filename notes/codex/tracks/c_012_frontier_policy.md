# C012 before-code policy: Zephyr frontier/contact events

**Design for root review; no implementation or calls authorized yet.** This follows [the strategic review](../strategic_review_20260909.md). The hypothesis is that scheduling source births and physical contacts together near a moving frontier can close low-degree owners as singletons, instead of introducing them far apart and repairing their chains later. One evolving physical assignment is maintained. B's whole-owner reconstruction is not used: C012 revises only proper terminal suffixes inside its current geometric window, preserves old original contacts at publication, and never returns to an expired strip. This is an unproved heuristic, not a novelty claim.

## Geometry and one sweep

Accept arbitrary simple undirected source graphs, including isolates/disconnected components. The target must have verified Zephyr `(u,w,k,j,z)` coordinates with parameters `(m,t)`; coordinates organize the search, but only supplied target adjacency certifies couplers. Missing sites/edges remain missing. Unsupported or inconsistent metadata is an explicit input failure, not a generic fallback constructor. Source labels, family metadata, saved maps and MM are never consulted.

Use one `Random(seed)`: draw one of four `(axis,reflection)` choices, then shuffle source insertion-order vertices and target insertion-order sites once to obtain immutable ranks. No later random draws or alternative sweeps. For the x axis define `s(q)=w` when u=0 and `s(q)=2z+j` when u=1; swap orientations for the y axis. Reflection replaces s by `2m−s`. Strip i contains the actual sites with s=i. The pinned [Zephyr edge loops](../../../.venv/codex-native/lib/python3.10/site-packages/dwave_networkx/generators/zephyr.py:194) imply `|s(a)−s(b)|≤2`; verify this on the supplied edges. Same-course edges extend z; odd and cross-orientation couplers are used only when actually present.

The birth window is `W_c={q:c≤s(q)≤min(c+2,2m)}`, starting at c=0. The carry window is `W_c ∪ W_(c+1)`. Three strips cover the maximum one-edge strip offset, **not every useful physical arrangement**. This width is a declared representational restriction, not an empirically calibrated necessity. Empty sites with s<c are expired and cannot be reclaimed. Owned sites there remain fixed. At most 2m cut advances occur; there is no restart or backward sweep.

## State and live obligations

State contains introduced source owners I, their nonempty disjoint connected physical chains, explicit construction trees, original-contact support counts, the current cut and immutable source/target ranks. After each publication **every source edge induced by I is realized**. Nonedges need no contact. Incidental original contacts count immediately.

Owner u is live exactly when `N_G(u)−I` is nonempty. A live chain must contain a site in W_c and have an actual adjacent **unused** site in the carry window. This is a one-port guard, not a proof of enough future capacity or jointly compatible free routes. Closed owners have all original neighbors introduced and all original contacts realized; their chain freezes immediately, with no compulsory continuation or cleanup. Isolates close as singletons. Singleton closure is supported, not guaranteed for every low-degree source.

The port guard prevents spending a live owner's last free neighbor on a leaf while more neighbors remain. It may reject a useful arrangement requiring temporary loss of access. Terminal exceptions are explicit: after introducing the last required neighbor an owner is closed and owes no free port. Completion of all source vertices owes no frontier invariant beyond full original-minor validity.

## Birth and contact scheduling

At a fixed state, order every unintroduced vertex by: most introduced neighbors; fewest still-unintroduced neighbors; source rank. Examine that finite list until a birth publishes. A success changes I/contacts and restarts the ordering; no success after the complete list triggers a cut advance. Time-interrupted lists remain censored, not exhausted.

For vertex v, choose its introduced neighbor with the smallest number of free adjacent sites in W_c as a pivot (source rank breaks ties). Candidate roots are those free sites; if there is no introduced neighbor, all free W_c sites are roots. A connected birth contained in W_c and touching that pivot necessarily contains such a root. Order roots by most directly touched introduced neighbor chains, most free neighbors in the carry window, smaller s, then target rank. No 32-root prefix or work-denial cap is imposed.

For each root, privately build a connected birth tree inside W_c. First test the singleton. If contacts are missing, repeatedly run a multi-source BFS from the current tree through unused W_c sites to a site adjacent to an uncovered introduced neighbor chain. Choose minimum added-site distance, then neighbor source rank and terminal target rank; fixed target-rank traversal breaks path ties. Add that path to v, update every incidentally covered neighbor, and continue. Other owners' sites are blocked. A failed BFS rejects this private root. No alternative path is explored for that root. Before accepting, check all introduced contacts, disjoint connectivity and the updated live-port obligations. Accept the first certified birth, not a minimum over successful outputs.

## Joint proper-suffix transaction

If every ordinary root fails, make one joint suffix attempt for that v, then continue the source list on failure. Eligible owners are **all live introduced neighbors of v** with a proper terminal suffix inside W_c; there is no arbitrary four-owner limit. This may be expensive and will be measured.

For each eligible owner's construction tree, its birth root and all sites outside W_c are protected. From each leaf, take the maximal path toward the root whose removed vertices are in W_c and have tree degree at most two; stop before a root, outside-window vertex or branch vertex. The nonempty path excludes that stopping anchor and must leave a nonempty connected remainder. Select one suffix per eligible owner: greatest leaf strip, then longest suffix, then leaf target rank. A singleton has no movable proper suffix. Physical chords do not invalidate the tree definition; actual remaining-chain connectivity is checked independently.

Privately release all selected suffixes together, retaining their owner/contact/tree records for rollback. Build v by the same ordered-root/BFS rule against the remaining chains. For each candidate birth, restore missing original contacts of the affected owners in source-rank order, growing from their connected remainders through currently unused W_c sites with the same nearest-uncovered-neighbor BFS. A previously restored contact must stay realized; no retained site of another chain moves. Full old induced-source contacts, the birth's contacts, disjoint connected chains and all live ports must pass before atomic publication. Otherwise discard the entire transaction, including any birth or restored prefix. Failure may reflect root/path/order restrictions, not lack of a feasible joint placement. Unselected owners and every outside-window site remain unchanged.

This complements birth scheduling; it never reconstructs a whole existing tree. Fixed roots, proper-path suffixes and closed-owner immobility are explicit neighborhood exclusions. No systematic backtracking, exact CSP, global optimizer or portfolio is present.

## Carrying, publication and stopping

When births are exhausted, carry live owners that would lack either an owned site in W_(c+1) **or a free port after the cut advances**. Membership alone is insufficient: an owner may otherwise lose its only port when empty strip-c sites expire. On the original carry-entry state, compute each endangered owner's reachable free destination sites in W_(c+1), through unused carry-window sites; order by fewest reachable destinations, shortest distance, then source rank. A zero set is a failed carry, not a global width theorem. Sequentially construct shortest paths in that order on one private occupancy map, recomputing actual paths after prior reservations. A destination qualifies only when adding its canonical BFS path gives that owner both next-window membership and a next-cut free port; test destinations in BFS order, without alternate paths to the same destination. There is no alternate carrying order. Validate all old contacts/chains plus next-window membership and next-cut free-port guards, then publish **all** carries and c+1 together. Any failure rolls back all carrying. Owners already reaching the next window with a surviving next-cut port need no added sites. Failure to advance ends this constructor; reaching the last window without completing births is `schedule_exhausted`.

Maintain exact local contact deltas and affected-owner connectivity at publication; final independent validation checks every original owner and original edge against the actual target. Attribute newly claimed sites separately to birth/contact creation, suffix restoration and frontier carrying; record released sites and signed net Q so nested events are not double-counted. Distinguish roots generated/rejected, BFS no-route, old-contact rejection, lost-port rejection, completed/censored transactions, suffix eligibility and source/cut exhaustion. Save initial/final maps, current I, missing original edges, live owners, current cut, counters and completed event receipts. Partial-contact states are not original minors.

Set absolute `D=min(start+timeout,supplied_deadline)`; next screens use root's common 60-second envelope. Reserve `min(.5,timeout/10)` seconds for final validation/private output. That reserve is administrative, not a guarantee of completion. Check the search deadline in every root/owner/BFS-edge loop, around sorting/copies and before publication. Integer site/edge/record counters are **diagnostic only**; no 19M/20M denial, local time slice or fixed pass cap. Preserve stage wall/CPU, full unsuccessful work and actual stop location. Search cutoff returns an uncredited failure; returning after D is late/TIMEOUT even if a diagnostic map validates. Unexpected exceptions remain ERROR and cannot be upgraded. The last published state survives private interruption; only a fully validated private final map completed by D can receive SUCCESS. No postfeasibility search or deletion phase is included in C012.

## Critique and first falsifier

C001/002 failed hard source/target splits; C003–006 preserved too much occupancy; C011's extra time did not fix clone/revision coordination. C012 removes those hierarchies and constant-Q assumptions but substitutes monotone strip exposure, immobile roots, greedy contact paths and a potentially wide live source frontier. Dense graphs/expanders may consume many carry sites or exhaust ports; sparse inputs can still lock because no closed owner can move. The tiny suffix mechanism cannot establish that these global restrictions are beneficial. Counting a port or finding individual carry routes does not prove a simultaneous frontier assignment.

After implementation authorization, targeted checks cover only new risks: (1) coordinate strip membership/edge classification and missing-coupler rejection; (2) full tiny path, branching tree, star with degree above target maximum and grid, including singleton closure and last-neighbor port release; (3) a private two-owner suffix/contact transaction, with rollback on lost contact or disconnected remainder and a singleton/root immobility negative; (4) a two-owner carry conflict proving atomic rollback, plus successful carry with exact net-Q attribution; (5) deadline during construction/certification/output, preserving the published map and fatal/late noncredit. Compare local deltas with the existing independent original-edge validator on those cases, without exhaustive enumeration. Measure reach and costs before accepting the window/suffix policy as an active default; a central failed primitive stops the implementation cycle for review.

A promising tiny gate goes directly to root's small complete panel: sparse/local, community, hub and dense fresh sources at two sizes, old diagnostic anchors/relabelings and evaluator-hidden feasible controls, with fresh MM and the retained A061 comparison. Root has frozen 22 inputs (20 structures plus two nested relabelings): six fresh families at n80/n160, grid/honeycomb/wheel anchors, K100, and four hidden-witness controls. Use C012/A061/MM, seed0, 60 seconds on hyde03. Root owns exact input identities, numerical continuation criteria, registry and lifecycle; no candidate receives evaluator witnesses. Require complete fresh-structure benefit and show every regression, Q/ACL, solver/process ratio and failure; partial contact progress is insufficient. Carry-dominated Q without complete cross-structure benefit rejects this policy. No universal 3× runtime gate, new graph generation, source implementation or constructor call is authorized by this document.
