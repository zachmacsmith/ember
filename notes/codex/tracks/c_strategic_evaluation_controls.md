# Proposed evaluation controls after C011

**Review proposal only: no inputs generated, witnesses constructed, solver called or new split frozen.** Keep one reviewed candidate against fresh stock MM. C011 removed work denial yet stayed at 2/8; further progress should be judged by complete original-valid embeddings and Q, with runtime roughly in MM's range. The former 3× screen threshold is not a user requirement. Controls below distinguish input sensitivity and known feasibility without adding a fallback or training the candidate on witness maps.

**Exposure audit.** [012](../experiments/012_corpus_selection.md) selected 485 inherited IDs, initially verified 481 and preserved four failures. [017](../experiments/017_corpus_readiness.md) selected 34 exact structures with 35 memberships; it links king/frustrated-square duplicates. C's eight sources were selected from that readiness set in the [initial C proposal](c_multilevel.md), then repeatedly reused through C011; [the final input manifest](../../../results/codex/track-c-011-protocol/preparation_manifest.json) binds the copies. These, the broader A/B development series, original corpus files, supplied partial-state diagnostics and corrected Sudoku q2/q3 are exposed development. Extra solver seeds, reordered nodes or renamed files do not create new structures. The earlier [fresh-instance review](../fresh_instance_evaluation_review.md) proposed generation and protection, but established neither. Its incomplete inherited identity index and missing files prevent a claim of global structural novelty.

Propose three separately reported development controls, with exact counts/settings subject to root review:

| Control | Small initial scope | Separating observation and limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Fresh structures | At each N=80,160: one fixed generation attempt for ER p=.10, BA m=4, regular d=8, WS k=6/beta=.2, four-block SBM (.20,.02), and the repository's full nonuniform-planar procedure. Add open grids 8×10/10×16, honeycombs (4,6)/(6,8), cubic grids (4,4,5)/(4,5,8). | Eighteen attempts sample multiple sparse/structured mechanisms and two scales without selecting outcomes. One instance/cell is a development screen, not a family mean; inherited matches remain linked development. Dense/all-family coverage remains outside this scope. |
| Label/order robustness | The same C8 structures, original solver encoding and one fresh fixed permutation; two independent solver seeds per encoding. Use identical encodings and seed indices for both methods. | A within-structure validity/Q shift exposes order sensitivity; it does not isolate tie breaking from downstream RNG effects. Do not pick the better permutation or count 16 independent graphs. |
| Hidden feasibility/Q witnesses | Four separately labeled controls: two singleton induced subgraphs of the actual Z12 record and two quotients of privately generated disjoint connected branch sets. Freeze construction/size/edge-retention rules before creation; retain generation failures. | Singleton witnesses certify Q=n optimality; multi-site witnesses give a feasible Q upper bound only. Failure establishes failure to find a known available solution, not which search component caused it. These target-derived sources are diagnostic supplements, not representative samples of Ember classes. |

Use the inspected [generator APIs](../../../scripts/generated_graphs/New_Generation/generate_graphs2.py:375), with their pinned source hash `f5528a0a…43b2`, NetworkX 3.4.2, exact parameters and generation caps frozen first. Actual lattice n/m, isolates and all failed attempts stay in the ledger; never substitute a partial planar result, another seed or a smaller graph. Fresh development starts with two solver seeds and one source permutation shared across methods. Labeling, generation, solver and execution-order seeds use independent namespaces; all outcomes, not minima across seeds, are retained.

Witnesses and source-to-target maps must reside in evaluator-only storage **outside the runtime bundle and candidate-readable filesystem**, not merely an unused function argument. Both solvers get only sanitized source adjacency, the common target, seed and fixed configuration. Remove source coordinates, family names, generation parameters, target-derived IDs and witness traces; preserve maps privately for the independent original-label validator. Hidden provenance cannot erase structural bias in target-derived sources. The current planted-source API loses its parent mapping during relabeling and its parent need not be Z12, so it does not supply these certificates unchanged.

**Untouched confirmation remains a separate future event.** Reuse the [generalization plan](../generalization_protocol.md) as a proposal, updating its exposure ledger through all A/B/C work before assignment. An independent evaluator should commit a salt hash and sealed generation specification, then create confirmation sources only after candidate, budgets and analysis are frozen. Developers must not see those graphs, failures, witnesses or outcomes before the complete frozen evaluation. Keep isomorphic duplicates and deterministic equivalents in one split; unresolved identity is `NOVELTY_UNRESOLVED`, not clean. Sudoku q4 and other previously proposed reservations require an exposure check before being called untouched. Finite families may have no fresh member; show that limitation instead of manufacturing relabeled replicates. Any opened confirmation data becomes development for later revisions.

Freeze a common wall-budget rule from existing development timing before new inputs; keep work counters observational unless a justified active cap is part of the algorithm. Charge all candidate stages, retain late-valid Q only diagnostically, and report missing/invalid/crashed calls in the denominator. Present success, common-valid Q/ACL comparisons and solver/process times separately; a success-conditioned quality table alone cannot establish overall superiority. No generation or benchmark follows automatically from this memo.
