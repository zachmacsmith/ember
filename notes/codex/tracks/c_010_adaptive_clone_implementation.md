# C010 implementation and focused checks

The standalone `adaptive_clones.py:clone_embed` implements the [fixed policy](c_010_adaptive_clone_policy.md). It starts with one clone per source vertex, greedily places individual sites, and revises only the bounded conflict block before one deterministic split. Domains use the whole target; the 32-site prefix restricts ranking only. Source order, one seeded target permutation, explicit original-edge IDs, internal owner-tree edges and placement timestamps are retained. No global assignment search, external embedder, A/B constructor, or cleanup is imported or called.

Only the three requested private check groups ran, using the isolated native interpreter. All passed in [check001](../../../results/codex/track-c-010-checks/check001/status.json), with 0.174 seconds total process time. They cover movement of an existing internal edge during splitting, independent owner-tree/original-edge checks, exact rollback of public placement/timestamps after failed/interrupted revision, and interrupted split publication. Exact-domain and seeded support-ranking checks distinguish the full 40-site domain from its 32 scored positions. A private path-placement fixture completes with Q=n. Three explicit legal triangle splits form a six-cycle with an independently valid original minor; this proves expressibility, **not** that the autonomous constructor finds that result.

The final gate independently recounts original physical contacts, membership, connectivity and overlap. Valid late maps stay diagnostic; invalid maps, incomplete clone placements and fatal errors receive no quality credit. After the three-group pass, I explicitly required absence of **any** retained error at credit time and added its affected assertion. Only the non-credit group was repeated: [noncredit002](../../../results/codex/track-c-010-checks/noncredit002/status.json) passed in 0.117 seconds process time. Earlier source/check bytes are preserved; no search policy changed.

Search and final work share a twenty-million bound, with nineteen million and an absolute 0.5-second reserve for search. Domain-word charges deliberately scale with full target size. Setup or repeated exact domains may therefore censor placement/splitting before useful progress; that is a cost result, not evidence of infeasibility. Nested revision/split walls are part of their parent visit, not additive solver-time categories. The remaining risks are fixed-prefix placement, restricted block scope and irrevocable partitions causing unnecessary Q or locked geometry.

Root requested a pre-screen cost calculation. [Saved-target arithmetic](../../../results/codex/track-c-010-checks/setup_cost.json), without importing/calling the constructor, gives **7,847,295 target-setup units** from 4,800 sites, 91,728 directed adjacencies and 75-word bitsets. Merely selecting/publishing 128 singletons costs at least **10,374,975 units including setup** (54.6% of search allowance). At most 8,625,025 remain for source-incidence scans, actual support ranking and revisions/splits; those omitted costs may exhaust the bound. This substantial fixed cost does not by itself prove that nineteen million is exhausted. No accounting rule or cap was changed. The lower bound uses only one candidate score per placement, since actual exact domains can have fewer than 32 sites.

No public constructor, corpus embedding, MM comparison or remote operation was executed. The [prepared screen](c_010_adaptive_clone_screen.md) awaits root's source/descriptor review and immutable freeze. C009 source/results remain unchanged.
