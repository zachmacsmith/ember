# Review and fixed initialization-only test of reserved access

The [reserved-access proposal](c_reserved_access_construction_proposal.md) is not structurally untenable before measurement. Its initial access guarantee is valid: a connected dominating reserve D in the fixed induced selection U leaves every nonempty occupied region adjacent to the same unused component. The main risks are the connectivity and contact capacity of O = U minus D, followed by consumption of that reserve during future routing. This test will address initialization only.

**Concrete tradeoffs.** Domination removes at least one incident edge from every occupied vertex. On full Z12 with maximum degree 20, occupied degree is therefore at most 19. A connected occupied chain of size k can have at most `17k + 2` outgoing occupied couplers, before accounting for duplicate contacts. Thus the complete source's degree 126 needs at least eight sites per chain to realize all contacts entirely inside O, versus the full-target lower bound of seven. This does not prohibit a smaller initial chain from growing through D later. Similarly, `|E(O)| - (|O| - n)` bounds distinct quotient contacts in any connected n-region partition of O; falling below source edge count proves that such a partition cannot already be a complete minor, not that future routing on the full target is impossible.

There is also an implementation-specific failure gate. The unchanged [`_assign`](../../../packages/ember-qc/src/ember_qc/algorithms/quotient_reconfiguration.py#L206) rejects a source vertex when no remaining region meets its existing full-target degree bound. Even if `|O| >= sum(lower)`, fragmentation and uneven coarsening can leave unusably small regions. Record the sorted region-size versus sorted lower-bound comparison separately: it determines whether any size-compatible assignment exists, without changing the greedy assignment or introducing an optimizer.

**Frozen inputs and procedure.** Use only the eight C006b records in the established order: 2030, 5058, 1584, 31536, 6450, 14334, 1041, 30736. Their saved active-set sizes are respectively 564, 484, 512, 608, 540, 1680, 3556, 484; all saved center ranks are 4512. Bind each original graph, target, saved initial map, active-rank list and result file through the existing C006b receipt. These exposed inputs remain development data.

Initialize `Random(0)` and replay only the unchanged [`_subset`](../../../packages/ember-qc/src/ember_qc/algorithms/quotient_compact.py#L19) to recover the baseline RNG position. Require its ordered active set and center to equal the saved values exactly; never enlarge, replace or retry the target selection.
2. Reserve the saved center. Repeatedly add the vertex in `N_U(D) minus D` maximizing newly dominated vertices in its closed neighborhood, breaking ties by full-target rank. Use exact maintained gains and a lazy versioned heap; each domination event updates affected neighbor gains. This implements the proposed greedy order without changing its objective. Save the full addition/gain trace and incomplete prefix on interruption.
3. Independently verify connected domination. Compute O and its components. Reject initialization if `|O| < n` or `components(O) > n`. Reindex O by ascending original target rank and use its actual induced adjacency. Run the unchanged C006 [`_coarsen`](../../../packages/ember-qc/src/ember_qc/algorithms/quotient_distinct.py#L51), then unchanged `_assign` with the original lower bounds and the post-subset RNG. Preserve any assignment failure; no size repair, fallback or additional seed is allowed.
4. Recount connected/disjoint regions, exact ownership O, initial original-source contacts, distinct quotient contacts/degrees, and full-target unused-component access from original edges. Verify every region touches D. Report reserve size, occupied components, lower-bound deficits, physical edge capacity, region sizes, quotient contacts and initial missing edges against the saved C006 initialization. Historical timings are not a comparator. Preserve all eight successes/failures/timeouts and every stage's work/cost.

**Cheap falsifier and self-critique.** Tiny exhaustive connected graphs should match a simple independent greedy reserve oracle exactly. Include a star whose reserve disconnects too many occupied components and a dense graph with a small successful reserve. Failure to form the prescribed n regions, assignment failure or diagnostic timeout falsifies this fixed initializer on that input. Otherwise the test measures the access/contact tradeoff; it cannot establish routing feasibility or justify a constructor automatically. Do not impose an arbitrary percentage-loss threshold after seeing outcomes. Any next routing policy requires separate review. No source-edge reconfiguration, path repair, deletion, complete constructor, MM or hardware change is included, and novelty remains unestablished.
