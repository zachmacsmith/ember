The isolated ordinary proposal adapter passes its bounded author checks and focused failure-accounting regression checks, and is ready for independent review. It does not change production search, native integration or any frozen experiment. Only tiny supplied synthetic minors were used; no constructor, corpus graph, competitor or remote process was called. The [accepted adapter specification](ordinary_proposal_diagnostic_adapter_spec.md) remains the behavioral basis.

Implementation: [scripts/codex/ordinary_proposal_diagnostic.py](../../scripts/codex/ordinary_proposal_diagnostic.py). Its public integration interfaces are:

```python
load_contact_repair(path) -> module
ordinary_batch(embedding, source, target, groups, *, deadline,
               max_expansions, contact_module, clock=None, cpu_clock=None)
    -> (candidate_or_none, info)
final_gate(candidate, entry, source, target, *, deadline,
           allowed_changed=None, expected_drop=None, clock=None)
    -> (private_candidate_or_none, gate_info)
validate_embedding(embedding, source, target, *, deadline=None, clock=None)
    -> dict(valid, reason, qubits, acl, max_chain)
```

`ordinary_batch` aliases `ordinary_first_contraction`. Graph arguments are supplied NetworkX graphs with simple undirected loopless integer topology. Group generation is external to this adapter but must remain inside the worker's overall arm deadline, including graph/adjacency preparation required by that generator. Import and immutable artifact decoding can be separate under root's protocol. `validate_embedding` with no deadline is intended for explicitly untimed evaluator input checks; it performs no search.

The source loader compiles exactly the bytes whose SHA256 matches the reviewed contact module, without executing Ember's package initializers or consulting bytecode caches. The required contact hash is `c6f9a5555b010d5e877b568c1ee760e72d170f1f7e0c186179de16b53630da85`. The actual worker must use this loader and check provenance. Explicit module injection is supported for synthetic checks; injected stubs lacking the source identity have `contact_source_sha256=None` and are not authorized runtime implementations.

The adapter makes one private entry copy, builds the ordinary context once and calls its entry validator once. Every group call receives that same entry, exact group order and unchanged `_repair`, with beam width 1, three alternatives, halo 2, region cap 512, two orders, 16 boundary sites, `qubits_contacts` and greedy trees. Each group's routing allowance is `min(50000, remaining_total)`; total work and the supplied absolute deadline never reset. Duplicate group occurrences remain separate. Within-group best selection is untouched; equal-Q output is diagnostic only. The first returned strict-Q candidate ends the batch, including when it saves more than one qubit. Exhausting the last routing unit does not invalidate an otherwise timely candidate.

There is **one common diagnostic final gate per positive arm output**. Ordinary invokes `final_gate` internally. The exchange worker invokes the same function on exchange's raw result, with `allowed_changed=proposal['selected']` and `expected_drop=1`. It must not invoke a second unconditional gate on ordinary output. Both arms retain their unchanged method-internal validation in addition. The common gate reconstructs target adjacency and owner contacts from the supplied original target edges, checks exact source coverage including isolates, target membership, disjoint connected nonempty chains, every original source edge, strict Q and outside-chain equality, and produces private lists for every owner. Its full measured cost belongs to the common deadline. Root's outer worker still needs a final deadline observation after its own subsequent bookkeeping/publication.

Per-group raw diagnostics, allowance, call wall/CPU, return time and result evidence are retained. Nonpositive ordinary returns use a selected-chain delta relative to the frozen entry, relying on the reviewed `_repair` guarantee that outside chains are unchanged; they do not receive another full-graph diagnostic scan. Their `q_basis` explicitly identifies the selected-size calculation. The first strict-Q return retains a complete mapping delta, including key order, added/removed keys and changed lists, before final validation; this preserves late and invalid positive proposals. Such evidence is uncredited unless the final gate and adapter's last clock check pass. `returned_contraction` is a raw Q-reduction flag, not an independent validity or timeliness claim. In particular, a raw late reduction may have that flag true and `credited_contraction=False`.

Input/graph/context snapshots are checked at arm boundaries. Full context and whole-entry mutation comparisons are not performed for every group. The original caller's chain lists are protected by the private entry copy. Snapshotting, per-group evidence, result copying and bookkeeping consume measured wall time, but do not fabricate ordinary routing expansions. These diagnostic overheads, the whole-target ordinary context construction and the occupied-state exchange setup differ and must remain visible; equal numerical work caps cannot establish equal compute. Setup, entry validation, raw proposal-call time and the final gate have separate wall fields; residual diagnostics/administration closes the total without summing nested times twice.

All failures preserve their status/evidence. A proposer exception records that the call occurred and its elapsed cost. `expansions` is explicitly the known trustworthy returned-record prefix; `routing_accounting_complete=False` and `expansions_exact=None` prevent reporting a raising call or malformed returned counter as exact zero/total work. `unaccounted_group_indices` identifies those occurrences. Setup, entry-validation and final-gate elapsed times are retained with `finally`, including exceptional exits. Final clock observations update wall and overrun even for an empty, failed or nonpositive arm; an error remains an error while `deadline_exceeded` records concurrent lateness. Neither internal accepted-move counters nor a late retained embedding earns contraction credit. Cooperative graph/copy checks still need the worker's external process backstop; this module is not a hard real-time guarantee.
