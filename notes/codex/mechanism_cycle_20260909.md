A's successful mechanism addresses a restriction in the **local proposal
representation**. Global connected branch sets already express general minors.
The earlier pure-path proposal omitted side-contact support; all26 unshortened
path questions become feasible with retained branches. Nine committed moves
save33 sites on cycle, kagome, grid and Petersen. Fresh A053 and the wrapper's
base Q agree on every input, so base variation cannot explain these savings.
This supplies end-to-end evidence across four exposed structures, not a
class-population claim. MM still has substantially shorter chains on five.

B's state representation can express valid embeddings, and the new move releases
the compulsory endpoints identified by B021. It makes474 commits and clears
overlap on only one input. Thus escaping the earlier locked neighborhood is
insufficient for this constructor. Of1124 queries,1017 complete;47 interrupted,
uncommitted queries discard230 otherwise eligible proposals. These receipts
identify a separate cost/publication restriction, but cannot establish a
counterfactual successful trajectory. A higher allowance or a wider local pool
is not justified by partial overlap reduction alone.

C likewise expands the move neighborhood: nine Q-neutral moves directly remove
twelve missing contacts. Six inputs first diverge through such a commit; regular
and complete have none, with their differences first appearing at a deadline
boundary. The additional grid completion is real, but the policy misses its
four-completion rule and all common MM quality comparisons. The private compound
routine costs0.459s of90.223s total; optimizing it cannot explain the broad
failure. There are3499 ordinary no-free-path visits, and shared BFS routines
examine408.1M adjacency entries. The latter is a work count, not measured BFS
time. Acceptance rejects none of the selected compound certificates.
