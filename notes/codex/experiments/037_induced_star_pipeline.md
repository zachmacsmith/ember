## Self-critique

Matching may find useful strict reductions but change later opportunities or
consume global work needed by ordinary groups. Its cache can disable when an
accepted ordinary move leaves insufficient maintenance allowance. Successful
ordinary equal-Q moves suppress attempts, while a greedy independent subset
can exclude a useful smaller block. All these are part of the fixed algorithm,
and their costs or regressions must remain visible.

The singleton-center restriction cannot address every high-degree chain.
Passing this screen would justify carrying one fixed revision forward and
testing new source instances; it would not satisfy the full research goal.
If the treatment does not improve cumulative quality at acceptable cost,
retain the complete negative evidence before revising it. Do not retune by
family, source identity or favorable seed, and do not publish a best-of score.
