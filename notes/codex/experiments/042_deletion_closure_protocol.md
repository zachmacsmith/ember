039 controls. Those certificates do not compose into 43 qubits of savings and
do not measure cleanup cost. The present hypothesis is that one deterministic

retained timely validity. This is conventional cleanup, not a novelty claim.

## Complete outcome and mechanism accounting

Publish all 34 paired rows with every status, original-graph validity, timely
eligibility, Q, ACL, maximum chain length, solver wall and process wall. Report
lower/equal/higher Q and ACL, total Q and arithmetic mean of per-input ACL on an
explicit common timely-valid population. Do not impute quality for unmatched
failures or treat a smaller late result as a primary win. Show those failures
and late results alongside the common population. Preserve every membership
without duplicate topology weight. A single seed supplies no across-run ACL
variance estimate; any reported input dispersion must be labelled accordingly.

Distinguish paired full-pipeline gain from within-treatment cleanup savings.
The extra enabled checks can consume time before construction, and timing can
alter contact coverage; equality of construction/refinement is an observation
to check, not a premise to impose on missing or binding runs. Compare constructed
Q, initial-pruned Q, contact savings/work/stop reason and cleanup entry Q on both
arms. Reconcile treatment entry Q minus accepted deletions with final Q. Retain
all contact diagnostics unchanged and all cleanup skip reasons, initial-check
and private-copy flags, alias behavior, closure completion, attempted/accepted
deletions, full accepted trace, phase wall, scan/connectivity/contact work and
known partial costs. Include expensive passes with no accepted deletion.

Independently validate every returned eligible embedding on both canonical and
original graph records. For a complete final embedding and cleanup trace,
reverse the recorded deletions to reconstruct entry chain sets and verify each
forward deletion's nonemptiness, connectivity, current logical contacts and
size/Q arithmetic against the original target. This can verify cleanup
intermediate states; it cannot recover unsaved contact-refinement states or
pre-cleanup list ordering. Missing traces, malformed arithmetic, invalid output
or incomplete provenance fail the relevant evidence gate rather than becoming
zero savings. Preserve diagnostic failures as explicit rows.

Separate wrapper wall from nested entry validation/module/phase wall; do not
double-count them in solver wall. Cleanup work is outside the contact routing
counter but inside the same time allowance. Report completed and interrupted
closures separately. A zero-saving completed closure establishes a local
single-deletion limit for that entry; a skipped/interrupted closure establishes
no such limit. The core's `closure_complete` alone is not proof that native
returned before its deadline. Historical 039/041 output differences may be
described with source/environment limits, but their timings are not concurrent
controls, and no result here is an MM comparison.

## Predeclared interpretation and critique

Judge the fixed treatment as a whole. Lower mean ACL with retained timely
validity supports replication; unchanged/worse mean, added failures or excessive
cost weighs against adoption. Do not promote it from positive within-closure
savings or the best affected graph, and do not choose a cleanup policy per
input. There is no numerical speed threshold inferred after outcomes; report
the measured timing distribution and all quality/reliability changes.

This screen is informed by the same development inputs, so it cannot establish
generalization. Initial validation may duplicate work without reducing Q;
deadline completion and cleanup's direct savings can disagree. Single-deletion
minimality does not imply minimum chain length, and order can preserve a poor
local minimum. The known 43 individual opportunities guarantee neither composed
savings nor savings in new full-pipeline calls. Positive evidence still requires
multiple seeds, new instances and a fresh same-host comparison against MM,
with a broader contribution assessment. No outcome alone establishes the
project's across-class objective.
