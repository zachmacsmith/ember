all successful and failed search, final independent result checking and private
output materialization. Carry the same absolute deadline through those stages.

immutable-input provenance checks and output serialization are recorded outside
that interval; the fresh-process measurement includes them. The graphs supplied
to a method contain only structure, without family or benchmark metadata.

Ordinary retains **500,000** total routing expansions, with each group receiving
`min(50,000, remaining)`. Exchange receives **1,250,000** elementary work units
for the entire batch, including its own setup and certificate. These are
different counters, not matched operation counts. Group preparation and common
final checking consume wall time but neither method's private work counter.

scaling targets about 1.25 seconds of estimated work within the five-second
allowance. This is an initial finite cap, not a runtime guarantee, a statistical
bound, or a calibration of Zephyr search depth. Local fixture throughput does
not establish hyde03 throughput or long-boundary cost. Binding limits must remain

## Decision and self-critique

Any exchange-only timely contractions justify studying this bounded mechanism
further, provided the full cost is recorded. They do not establish strict
neighborhood containment, useful cumulative gain or novelty. If no exchange
proposal is returned, distinguish completed searches from binding limits before
deciding whether to revise the mechanism or its allocation. Do not replace a
negative result with an enlarged-cap rerun under this experiment identity.

The four-stream rule retains ordinary three/four-chain opportunities but changes
its usual priority and excludes original patches larger than 64. Ownership can
admit additional owners; ordinary can use unoccupied sites. First group return
latency differs from first internal discovery. The inherited entry may favor or
hinder either move system. Five seconds is an exploratory proposal allowance,
not a demonstrated sustainable addition to MM-scale embedding runtime. All 34
structures have been used repeatedly in development. A successful mechanism
still requires a fixed integration design, complete-pipeline comparison,
elementary-exchange controls and fresh-instance validation before any broad
quality or publication claim.
