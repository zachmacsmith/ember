## Decision and self-critique

The recurrence correction has a validity/optimality argument for its restricted
class-interval problem. A corrected assignment can change seating, completion,
pruning and later contacts, so it need not improve final ACL. More seated arms
may increase raw qubits while eliminating an incomplete conversion; that is
coverage, not a quality regression by itself. Final valid qubits are decisive.

Keep all regressions, failures and time overruns. A substantial state/time
increase in035requires review before launch; no arbitrary DP state cap may be
silently inserted into an exactness claim. The pipeline watchdog bounds exposure
but does not turn a late call into timely success. Correct class feasibility
does not fix missing endpoints, defective lanes or every converter limitation.

Do not use this result to choose old versus corrected conversion per input.
Any next revision is one globally fixed algorithm. If the corrected solver
reduces final quality broadly, investigate the mechanism or replace the class
solver with a separately specified feasible heuristic; do not hide the known
capacity defect inside a preferred-family exception.
