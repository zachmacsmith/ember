of generalization, and seed 0 cannot estimate run-to-run variance. Changed

bounded falsifier: lost success, a Q regression, or gains confined to previously
screened instances refute its specified transfer claim. Do not replace this
with more receipt counting. All 66 calls finish even after a regression appears.

runtime deficit without an artificial universal ratio gate. Broader classes,
additional independent structures, repeated seeds and untouched confirmation
remain required before the final research objective can be claimed.
