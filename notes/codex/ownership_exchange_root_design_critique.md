# Root critique of deletion-conditioned ownership exchange

2026-09-08 UTC. Root read the complete mechanism design and primary-source
review. The design remains a hypothesis, not implementation approval. Ordinary
transfers, swaps, missing contacts and repairing an infeasible intermediate
state are established techniques. The possible contribution is the particular
contraction search and useful reductions per cost beyond existing reconstruction.
Its hand witness proves that one simultaneous exchange repairs a deletion; it
does not prove the existing proposer misses that improvement.

The strongest risks are limited reach and unsuccessful work. Occupied-only
moves cannot exploit an unused shortcut. Connected intermediate chains and the
four-successor/depth-eight limits can exclude needed repair sequences. Failed
generation, owner admission, copying and validation can consume the allowance
before any useful candidate exists. Every such operation must be charged and
interruptible at stated boundaries. A strict local reduction is not proof of
better final pipeline quality or roughly MM-scale runtime.

Root does not accept the design's proposed wholesale replacement of ordinary
reconstruction as a settled integration choice. That would discard useful
free-space moves without evidence. Multiple local moves acting on one incumbent
can form one non-portfolio heuristic; integration should follow measured reach
and cost. No parallel best-result selection or extra constructor is authorized.

Next work is a precise isolated API and correctness specification. Retain the
untuned proposed caps: eight whole chains, 64 incumbent occupied sites, eight
exchanges per branch, four successors and 256 visited assignments per seed.
Define ownership partition, contact completeness, connected/nonempty chains,
undo and admitted-owner behavior, interrupted successor selection, one absolute
deadline and final atomic publication. Each returned proposal must be globally
valid and occupy exactly one fewer qubit. No free sites, IP or external embedder
enter this API. Specification and self-critique precede implementation.

Before a later mechanism diagnostic, root will specify all 34 own-algorithm
control incumbents and independently close their easy deletions. Such saved
incumbents are diagnostic inputs only, not inputs to a complete embedding
algorithm. Fix ordinary-neighborhood comparison budgets before execution, keep
all failed queries, and measure extra contractions and their full cost. Compare
bounded elementary exchanges before making any contribution claim. No corpus
diagnostic or native integration has yet been authorized for this mechanism.
