# What the pinned stock MM actually does

The comparator is stock **minorminer 0.2.22**, not the mutable `external/minorminer-fork` checkout. Saved host02/Python3.10 and host06/Python3.11 receipts match their official PyPI wheels exactly for the compiled embedding extension, public wrapper, initialization module, Cython interface, METADATA and WHEEL (seven files each); their corresponding installed RECORD entries also match. The eleven reviewed files extracted from upstream tag `0.2.22`, commit `e27ec28fcf731f7e85df5b107596880202bc703d`, equal the official source distribution. [Distribution verification](/Users/dabh/ember/results/codex/stock-mm-strategy-review/host_distribution_comparison.json) records every hash and download URL; [PyPI release](https://pypi.org/project/minorminer/0.2.22/) identifies the package. This verifies distributed bytes, not a reproducible rebuild of the binary. No MM import or embedding call was made for this review.

The pilot calls public `find_embedding` with seed, timeout and empty configuration. Its Python wrapper explicitly supplies `tries=10`, `max_no_improvement=10`, **`chainlength_patience=10`**, and `threads=1`; the C++ constructor's fallback patience of two is not the effective comparator default. Initial/fixed/restricted chains are empty. This call does not invoke the clique/busclique constructor. [Exact wrapper](https://github.com/dwavesystems/minorminer/blob/0.2.22/minorminer/minorminer.py#L23).

| Implemented capability | Concrete operation and plausible relevance |
|---|---|
| All-neighbor root choice | Remove the visited chain; compute weighted shortest-path maps from every already embedded neighbor, sum root distances, and choose among minimum roots with seeded ties. Default domains cover the processed target component—on connected ideal Z12, the whole target. A fixed small root pool does not impose our B experiments' restriction. The estimate is not exact minimum union size. [Root search](https://github.com/dwavesystems/minorminer/blob/0.2.22/include/find_embedding/pathfinder.hpp#L347), [distance accumulation](https://github.com/dwavesystems/minorminer/blob/0.2.22/include/find_embedding/pathfinder.hpp#L722). |
| Shared connecting branches | `construct_chain_steiner` connects successive neighbors from suitable junctions already in the new tree, allowing paths to share owned branches. It does not simply sum independent physical arms, nor solve Steiner tree exactly. This can matter for hub and clustered neighborhoods. [Construction](https://github.com/dwavesystems/minorminer/blob/0.2.22/include/find_embedding/embedding.hpp#L188). |
| Movable contact boundaries | `steal_all` can absorb contact-only pendant segments from neighbors before rebuilding a chain; `flip_back` redistributes such segments after routing. `chain::steal` updates both endpoint links, uses reference-counted leaf trimming, and leaves the donor nonempty. Neighbor chains and contact locations can therefore change during what looks like a one-vertex visit. This is absent from B022's frozen outside chains and B023's route-only growth. [Boundary operations](https://github.com/dwavesystems/minorminer/blob/0.2.22/include/find_embedding/embedding.hpp#L219), [transfer details](https://github.com/dwavesystems/minorminer/blob/0.2.22/include/find_embedding/chain.hpp#L285). |
| Temporary overlap with structured progress | Feasibility passes reconstruct connected chains using exponential costs based on current site occupancy. Best-state comparison first minimizes the maximum occupancy and then its histogram. Current state can change without improving the saved best; this is not B025's mandatory per-action nonincrease in a fixed weighted sum. [Weights](https://github.com/dwavesystems/minorminer/blob/0.2.22/include/find_embedding/embedding_problem.hpp#L286), [comparison](https://github.com/dwavesystems/minorminer/blob/0.2.22/include/find_embedding/pathfinder.hpp#L142). |
| Restructuring after feasibility | With overlap forbidden, `find_short_chain` expands searches from all neighbor chains and evaluates actual constructed trees at reached common roots, retaining/reverting a candidate as needed. Repeated chain-length passes continue until patience or timeout, with patience reset after improvement. They can relocate chains and move contact boundaries; B023 stops at first feasibility followed by leaf deletion. [Actual-size search](https://github.com/dwavesystems/minorminer/blob/0.2.22/include/find_embedding/pathfinder.hpp#L374), [quality phase](https://github.com/dwavesystems/minorminer/blob/0.2.22/include/find_embedding/pathfinder.hpp#L674). |

Stock's valid-state objective is the descending chain-length histogram, not total Q: a profile `[2,2,2]` can beat `[3,1,1]` while increasing Q. Initialization/feasibility ordering uses graph traversal with seeded ties; stalled feasibility attempts may restart, up to ten trials under one timeout. This does **not** establish ten starts occurred in a saved comparator call, and no best-output portfolio is proposed here. Quality starts after feasibility; best valid state is preserved. [Histogram](https://github.com/dwavesystems/minorminer/blob/0.2.22/include/find_embedding/embedding.hpp#L272), [trial control](https://github.com/dwavesystems/minorminer/blob/0.2.22/include/find_embedding/pathfinder.hpp#L589).

These capabilities are causal hypotheses for several gaps, not causes proved on our samples. The saved MM maps do not expose which roots, transfers, restarts or quality passes produced them. Root's independent A061 comparison of grid, honeycomb, BA, wheel, LFR, planted-partition and planar maps should be read separately. In particular, a native core's surplus cannot automatically be blamed on later lifting, and a small final deletion count does not exclude profitable contact relocation.

The strongest coherent direction is **whole-chain replacement with movable neighboring contact branches**, used throughout one evolving construction and quality trajectory. In pseudocode: choose logical vertex `u`; privately release its tree and the neighbors' branches used only for their contact with `u`, preserving every other neighbor obligation; connect `u` jointly to the remaining neighboring contact regions using shared paths; redistribute connecting stems and remove branches made unnecessary; recertify changed chains and original contacts, then atomically accept or roll back. Before feasibility, overlap remains a measured defect; afterward, retain a valid incumbent and seek smaller actual Q while preserving disjointness. No independent starts or inherited constructor are required. Exact scheduling/acceptance still needs a separate design decision; B025 rules out treating simple current-energy nonincrease as an established solution.

A hand example isolates the missing operation: embed source path `a–b–c` in target path `0–1–2–3–4` as `{a:[0], b:[1,2,3], c:[4]}`. It is deletion-minimal, and frozen-neighbor single-chain replacement cannot reduce Q. Absorb `b`'s contact-only stem `1,2` into `a`, then rebuild `a` at `2`: `{a:[2], b:[3], c:[4]}` has Q3 instead of Q5. This demonstrates the boundary-transfer mechanism, not reach beyond all existing A ownership moves, which may realize the same change sequentially.

Self-critique: this is established minor-embedding territory, not a novelty claim. Pendant-only release can still lock useful shared branches, and unrestricted all-neighbor searches cost roughly `degree(u) × (|E_target| + |V_target| log |V_target|)` per visit with sizable distance storage. Stock uses compiled arrays/queues; a Python transcription need not operate at its scale. Geometry may support exact reusable distance/frontier primitives, but that is an implementation opportunity rather than permission to impose another target-region restriction. A future complete-constructor comparison must record neighboring sites released, realized Q reductions after feasibility, defect clearance, and routing/quality time. If those moves never occur, the neighborhood was not exercised; if they occur without end-to-end gains, this mechanism is insufficient. A failed distance proxy or an overlap decrease alone must not sustain another refinement series.

This review proposes a strategic mechanism only. B025 remains retired; no candidate code, new local query, solver run, or new screen was performed.
